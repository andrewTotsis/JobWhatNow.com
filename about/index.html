About

An independent take on jobs and hiring.

No course funnels. No sponsored content. No recycled advice. Just honest, data-backed coverage of what's actually happening.

What we publish

JobWhatNow covers the modern job market as it actually is — including the parts that are uncomfortable to say. We focus on four lenses: Insights (long-form analysis), Jobs (role-by-role hiring breakdowns), Trends (where the market is moving), and Guides (tactical playbooks).

Editorial standards

  • Cite or don't claim. Every number we publish links to its source.
  • Interview real people. Recruiters, hiring managers, and candidates — anonymized when needed.
  • Update what's outdated. Each piece carries a "last updated" date and we revisit when reality changes.
  • Disclose, always. If we recommend a tool, we say whether we have any relationship with it.

How we make money

Honestly: this site is small and the cost of running it is low. Over time, we may earn affiliate fees from a small number of tools we'd recommend anyway. Those will always be disclosed in-line. We don't run sponsored articles. We don't accept payment for coverage.
